What Family Hub is: A mobile app to help neighbouring families stay connected and care for their shared home.

Why it exists: Living close to one another does not automatically make everyday coordination easy. Families need a way to share updates, remember community events, organise shared maintenance, and keep track of contributions without important information being missed.
Family Hub would bring these needs into one shared mobile app for a small community. It would start with the four families currently living on the premises, with a longer-term vision of serving a close-knit community of five to ten families.

Who it's for: The initial community has four families and 14 members: eight adults, three children over 10, and three children under 10. The app is intended for parents and children, with shared information for families and enjoyable activities across age groups.

Key features:
1.	Login: Give community members a way to access Family Hub.
2.	Shared calendar: Let families see upcoming community events in one place.
3.	Important activity leaderboard: Highlight important community activities. Before building, decide whether this means a priority board or a ranked leaderboard.
4.	Daily fun quiz: Offer a shared activity that different age groups can enjoy.


Current roadmap:
Goal: Build a simple first version where members can log in, view upcoming events, find important activities, and enjoy a daily quiz.
Phase	What to do	Ready to move on when…
1. Define the first version	Decide who can join, who can add events, who manages important activities, and who supplies quiz questions. Clarify the “leaderboard” concept.	Each feature has a clear purpose and a short list of requirements.
2. Sketch the user journey	Outline the login, home, calendar, important activities, and quiz screens. Show how a member moves between them.	Potential users can understand the screens and find each feature.
3. Build the core	Implement login and the shared calendar first. Include viewing events and a simple way for an authorised member to add or update them.	A member can log in and see an event added by another member.
4. Add activities and the quiz	Build the important activities screen, then the daily quiz. Keep the first quiz format simple: a question, answer choices, and a result.	Members can find an important activity and complete a quiz.
5. Pilot and improve	Let a small group try all four features. Record confusing steps, errors, and missing information. Fix the essentials before expanding.	Users can complete the main tasks without assistance.
6. Release the first version	Make the app available to its intended members and establish who maintains events, activities, and quiz content.	The app has a clear content owner and a manageable list of improvements.
